Low-rise / Thonglor / duplex

One home,
two floors, one staircase.

A 180.49 sqm duplex on the fourth and fifth floors of a low-rise on Thong Lo 25, joined by an open staircase.

Duplex / W8 Thonglor, Unit 88/86

Where the staircase stays open.

On the fourth and fifth floors of W8 Thonglor, this duplex works the way its plan intended: living and dining downstairs, a landing above, and a staircase that is open between them.

It sits in the same building, and has the same owner, as the combined unit at 88/88, whose own staircase is currently closed so that the floors can be let as two homes. Here the staircase is open, so the home reads as one continuous space across two levels. The owner is also the owner of the building.

The mood is warm and woody. Timber floors run throughout, with dark joinery, louvred timber blinds, glass-sided stairs and a kitchen of light timber and white tile. The living area holds a day bed, a massage chair and a long dining table, and the windows look out across the neighbouring rooftops and trees. The building accepts dogs up to 20 kg.

The number of bedrooms is still to be confirmed. It is offered for sale only, not for rent. The photographs show several bedrooms and three bathrooms.

Story drawn from the owner’s description, relayed by the listing team, plus the supplied photographs. Not yet reviewed by the owner.

Room to sit, eat and spread out.

A long timber dining table, sofas arranged around a low table and a day bed under the windows share one open room. The ceiling steps down in a recessed border with downlights.
