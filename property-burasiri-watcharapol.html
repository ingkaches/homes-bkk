House / Burasiri Watcharapol, No. 55/71

Room for everyone, and a lawn to run on.

A house shows how a family lives in it. Here it is a children’s table in a corner of the living room, a guitar leaning by a bed, a golf bag in the study and a long workbench in the carport.

The house is at No. 55/71 in Burasiri Watcharapol, a Sansiri house project in O Ngoen, Sai Mai, completed around 2018 and listed with a swimming pool, gym and security among its facilities. It has five bedrooms and five bathrooms over two storeys, 288 sqm indoors, on a plot of 494.80 sqm, or about 123 square wah. That plot is the quiet luxury of a house like this: a real lawn, a covered terrace that opens onto it, and room for the cars to stay under cover.

The day starts in a kitchen of timber and white, with a breakfast bar for the quick coffee and a dining table that seats eight for everything else. The ground floor is for company: a chandelier over the living room, a leather sofa facing the media wall, a study off the hall for the one person who has to work. Upstairs, a second lounge with its own balcony lets the household split into two evenings, and the bedrooms gather around the landing.

The family who own it are Thai, and they are relocating, so the house is now for sale. They have not shared more than that and we have not guessed at it. What a buyer sees is a house that was clearly used as a home, with space to keep using it as one.

Story drawn from the owner’s description, relayed by the listing team, plus the supplied photographs. The reason for the relocation has not been supplied and is not stated. Not yet reviewed by the owner.

A living room for company.

The ground floor is built for hosting. A chandelier hangs over the living room, a leather L-shaped sofa faces a media wall, and the room runs straight into the dining area. A small children’s table in the corner shows how it has been used.

03

A kitchen, a table for eight and a study.

The kitchen is timber and white with a gas hob and a black double-door fridge. A breakfast bar with stools separates it from a dining table that seats eight. Off the hall, a quiet ground-floor room is set up as a study, with a long built-in counter and an office chair.

An upstairs lounge, with its own balcony.

The first floor has a second sitting room, with a slatted media wall, an L-shaped sofa and sliding glass onto the balcony, so a family can keep two evenings going at once.

Bedrooms, a walk-in wardrobe and bathrooms to match.

The main bedroom has a tufted headboard and a dressing table. A walk-in wardrobe has hanging space for a whole household, and the bathrooms are done in pale stone-effect tiles with generous basins.
