A high-floor home beside the BTS at Wongwian Yai, with a long balcony and a skyline that opens up as soon as you walk in.

High-floor home / Nye by Sansiri

The owner bought it for the sky.

Most buyers choose a condo for the layout. This owner chose it for the view: a high floor, a wide open balcony, and, by the owner’s account, a panorama that fills with fireworks at New Year.

Nye by Sansiri is a Sansiri development completed in 2014, with two towers and 636 homes beside BTS Wongwian Yai. The owner describes a Chao Phraya River view from this home and says fireworks can be seen from here not only on New Year’s Eve but at other times of the year. The photographs show a high, open skyline; the river sightline is the owner’s account and is worth checking at a viewing.

Inside, the home is dressed in cream built-in cabinetry, mirrored panels, a black leather sofa and a bedroom wall in navy and brick. It has also been a good earner. The owner says it rents well, including on shorter stays, and that the building is well maintained.

Now the owner feels they have invested enough. They want to return to their hometown, retire and run a small food business, so the home is for sale.

Story and rental comments are the owner’s account, relayed by the listing team. Not yet reviewed by the owner. View, floor and rental performance to be verified at viewing.

A balcony built for watching.

The balcony runs along the corner of the living room, behind tall glass doors and a window seat. In the photographs the city spreads out low and wide beneath the clouds and glows at sunset. The owner says this is where the New Year fireworks are watched from.

“A home chosen for what you can see from it.”From the editors
02

A living room in cream and leather.

White built-in cabinetry wraps one wall of the living room, with a built-in desk, display shelves and a chandelier overhead. Mirrored panels bounce the light around, and the black leather sofa is set to face the glass.

03

A compact kitchen and a table for four.

The kitchen is a tidy galley with dark cabinetry, a stone-effect worktop and a washing machine built in. A wooden dining table sits in front of display cabinets, between the kitchen and the living area.

04

A bedroom in navy and brick.

A tufted headboard sits against a painted brick panel framed in navy, with wall lights either side and checked cushions. A television and wall shelves are arranged along the window wall.

Bathrooms, simply done.

Light tiles, a dark floating vanity and a glass-screened shower with a window that lets daylight in.
