Riverside / The Residences at Mandarin Oriental

A river home,
kept for the big nights.

An eleventh-floor residence on the Chao Phraya, attached to ICONSIAM, used by its owner only once or twice a year for the New Year fireworks.

Riverside home / Unit 289/21

Quiet all year, and then the whole river lights up.

This home is lived in only once or twice a year. Its owner comes to Bangkok around the holidays and the New Year, for one reason above all: the fireworks over the river.

The Residences at Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok stands on Charoen Nakhon Road, right on the Chao Phraya and connected to ICONSIAM. The 52-storey tower was completed in 2019 and holds 146 homes. The project lists hotel-style services for residents, among them a doorman and car valet, housekeeping and laundry, and a personal chef on request. Fees and availability are for the viewing team to confirm.

Inside, the palette is pale stone, dark bronze and soft leather. The living room faces the water and opens onto a long balcony, and the bedrooms sit further back, where it is quieter. Because the home is used so rarely, it is presented exactly as photographed: furnished, styled and ready for the next owner to arrive with a suitcase.

The owner now wishes to sell. For a buyer who wants a place on the river for the nights that matter, or a Bangkok base that stays ready between visits, this is the case for it.

Owner’s use of the home relayed by the listing team; project details from the developer’s public project information. Not yet reviewed by the owner.

01

The river is the main room.

From the eleventh floor the Chao Phraya fills the windows: boats below, the city skyline beyond, and ICONSIAM’s riverfront at your feet. The balcony runs the length of the living space, so the view is part of the room rather than something behind glass.

“A home kept for the nights the whole river is lit.”From the editors
02

A living room with a sense of occasion.

Crystal chandeliers, black-framed wall panelling, a marble-effect rug and a long dining table for six make the main rooms feel dressed for company. A glowing display wall with lit shelves anchors the space beside the television and fireplace surround.

03

A kitchen kept discreet.

The kitchen is set apart from the living room behind a glazed bronze screen: long bronze-toned cabinetry, a stone worktop and a window that brings the river light in. It is a working kitchen designed to stay out of sight when guests arrive.

04

Bedrooms set back from the water.

Both bedrooms have upholstered headboards, dark wood and stone accents, and tall curtains. One keeps the river in view from the bed; the other opens toward the balcony.

05

Stone, steam and a long soak.

Bathrooms are finished in pale marble with black inlays. One shows a double vanity leading to a soaking tub framed by a window; another has a stone basin and a glass shower.
