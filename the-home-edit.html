Before you submit

Questions owners ask.

Does it cost anything?

Submitting is free. Selected homes receive extra presentation; we will tell you exactly what that involves before anything is published.

Who sees my details?

Your contact details are only for our team. Exact addresses and unit numbers stay private until a viewing is confirmed.

Do you only list the best homes?

We would rather show fewer homes properly. Every home needs real photographs and honest facts, and anything unknown is marked pending.

Can I see how a home is presented first?

Yes. Start with the Tristan Condo story, or browse all homes.
