Two homes now.
One five-bedroom later.

A 221.59 sqm combined unit across the top two floors of a low-rise building on Thong Lo 25, set up as two residences and ready to be one.

Low-rise home / W8 Thonglor, Unit 88/88

A staircase that is closed, by choice.

This unit spans the sixth and seventh floors of W8 Thonglor. It was drawn with a staircase between the two floors, but the owner has closed it and set the floors up as two separate homes.

The reason is simple. The owner is also the owner of the building, and they decided that two residences, a three-bedroom and a two-bedroom, would work harder as an investment than one large home. Each has its own living room, kitchen and bedrooms, with city views through large panes of glass. Today the floors can be rented separately, at ฿65,000 a month for floor 6 and ฿80,000 for floor 7, or the whole unit can be bought for ฿25M.

A buyer can take the arrangement as it is, or take the staircase back. The owner says the two floors can be connected again to make a single five-bedroom home of 221.59 sqm. W8 Thonglor is a low-rise on Thong Lo 25, a lane off Sukhumvit in Watthana, and the building accepts dogs up to 20 kg.

The photographs show two distinct interiors: a living room with a terracotta sofa and a mint-glass kitchen, and another with a round white sofa and a grey kitchen. We have not yet matched each set of photographs to the three-bedroom or the two-bedroom residence.

Story drawn from the owner’s description, relayed by the listing team, plus the supplied photographs. Not yet reviewed by the owner.
