# HOMES/BKK Public Experience Prototype

## Design thesis

**A warm city magazine you can act on.**

This four-page public prototype turns the business model into an original experience:

- **Warm editorial base:** cream paper, muted moss, soft natural light, airy typography.
- **Living Gallery:** collections are issues with curator notes—not renamed filters.
- **Bangkok After Rain:** a single occasional cinematic chapter, used as rhythm rather than the global colour scheme.
- **Marketplace clarity:** every beautiful moment resolves into facts, availability, a clear source, and a next action.
