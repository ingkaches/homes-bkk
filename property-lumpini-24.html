Decorated home / The Lumpini 24

Made to feel like someone lives here.

Some homes are furnished to be neutral. This one was furnished to be liked: oak shelves, a black metal frame in the shape of a little house, a marble-topped dining table and a mirror tall enough to lean against the wall.

The owner decorated it with care and has kept it in good order. Now they are retiring and want to let the property go, so the home is for sale.

The Lumpini 24 is an LPN development on Sukhumvit 24, with two towers, 402 homes and a pool and gym on the upper floors. The nearest BTS station, Phrom Phong, is about 0.9 km away. From this home the windows look over a wide spread of rooftops to a skyline that includes a tall orange-red tower.

The size and the exact layout are still to be confirmed. The photographs show a living and dining room, a kitchen, a bedroom with a view and a bathroom with a freestanding tub.

Story drawn from the owner’s message, relayed by the listing team, plus the supplied photographs. Not yet reviewed by the owner.
