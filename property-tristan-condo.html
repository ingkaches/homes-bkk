A furnished two-bedroom with practical kitchen flow, warm wood floors and an everyday living room that is clearly made for both people and pets.

Cover home / Tristan Condo, Unit 11/16

Made over, not just found.

The owner works in Thailand and had a short list: a condo in the heart of the city, quiet enough to come home to, and a building that would welcome a dog. This building was the one that fit.

The unit did not look like this when they arrived. The owner invested in renovating and designing the whole home, and what you see in these photographs is the result: framed art on almost every wall, plants in the corners, a guitar waiting on its stand beside the kitchen counter, and warm wood tones running through every room.

It has been lived in with one dog, and it shows: pet bowls beside the counter, a dog stretched out on the living-room rug. Now the owner is moving to a smaller place that suits living alone, and this home is looking for its next household.

Story drawn from a conversation with the owner, relayed by the listing team, plus the supplied photographs. Not yet reviewed by the owner.

Light, and room to spread out.

Where cooking and company meet.

White cabinetry, a tiled splashback and a wooden worktop make a kitchen that is easy to use. A few steps away, a wooden dining table is set with grey moulded chairs, under paintings that bring colour into an otherwise calm room.

03

A second room that works for a living.

The second bedroom is currently arranged as a home office: a compact white desk set up for two laptops, a shelf above it, and a sofa and wardrobe sharing the same room.

A quiet end to the day.

The bedroom is dressed in dark tones against white walls, with the bathroom kept bright and clean beside it. The planted balcony is the last room in the story: a green corner above the city.
